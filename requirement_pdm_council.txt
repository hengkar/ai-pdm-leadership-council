# AI PDM Leadership Council

## Core Idea

Junior and mid-level Product Managers often encounter situations they have never seen before.

Example:

> “My engineering team keeps pushing back on my roadmap. What should I do?”

Instead of generic advice, the system retrieves relevant thinking from experienced product leaders.

Possible knowledge sources include:

- Lenny Rachitsky
- Shreyas Doshi
- Julie Zhuo
- Gibson Biddle
- Teresa Torres
- Marty Cagan
- Brian Chesky
- Elena Verna
- Casey Winters
- other experienced product leaders

## Example Response

### Your Situation

Engineering disagrees with roadmap priorities.

### Perspective 1

Clarify whether the disagreement is about:

- expected impact
- execution complexity
- product strategy
- technical risk
- prioritization

### Perspective 2

Bring engineers earlier into discovery rather than presenting a completed roadmap.

### Recommended Actions

1. Run a problem-framing session.
2. Share customer evidence.
3. Ask engineers to propose alternative solutions.
4. Agree on success metrics before debating implementation.
5. Separate disagreement about the problem from disagreement about the solution.

## Product Modes

### Ask an Expert

The PM can select a specific expert.

Examples:

- Ask Shreyas
- Ask Elena
- Ask Brian
- Ask Teresa

### Ask the Product Council

Multiple expert perspectives are retrieved and compared.

Example:

> “How would three experienced product leaders approach this problem differently?”
